About

Good people strategy only works when it fits the operation around it. I work where HR policy, workforce planning and business analysis meet.

I started my career in education in Dhaka, where I learned to coordinate teams, communicate with families and handle confidential information with care. I then moved into HR and operations in the UK, after completing an MSc in International Business with Human Resource Management at Ulster University.
