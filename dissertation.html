Employee perceptions of work-life balance: remote vs in-office roles

Research into how employees in multinational corporations experience work-life balance, and what it means for HR strategy, wellbeing and hybrid work.

01Business or people challenge

Many organisations now run remote, hybrid and in-office roles side by side. Employees in these arrangements may experience work-life balance differently, which matters for wellbeing, engagement and retention. The question: how do employees in multinational corporations perceive their work-life balance in remote versus in-office roles?

02Context

MSc dissertation for the MSc in International Business with Human Resource Management, Ulster University (Birmingham), completed 2025. Scope: multinational corporations. This is an independent academic project, not an employer assignment.

03Objective

To understand how employees perceive work-life balance in remote versus in-office roles, and to draw out implications for HR strategy, wellbeing and hybrid work practices.
